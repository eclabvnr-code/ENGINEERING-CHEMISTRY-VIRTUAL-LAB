Principle & Reaction Mechanism: Preparation of Nylon-6,6 (Interfacial Polymerization)

Principle: Interfacial Step-Growth Condensation

Nylon-6,6 is a linear thermoplastic polyamide prepared by condensation polymerization of hexamethylenediamine (a 6-carbon diamine) and adipoyl chloride (a 6-carbon diacid chloride). In the laboratory "Nylon Rope Trick," the two monomers are dissolved in two immiscible solvents—hexamethylenediamine with NaOH in water (dense lower aqueous layer) and adipoyl chloride in cyclohexane (less dense upper organic layer).

Chemical Reaction Equation

n H₂N—(CH₂)₆—NH₂  +  n Cl—CO—(CH₂)₄—CO—Cl  →  —[NH—(CH₂)₆—NH—CO—(CH₂)₄—CO]ₙ— + 2n HCl

Role of NaOH: Neutralizes the liberated HCl byproduct (HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O), preventing protonation of the —NH₂ groups and driving polymerization continuously at the interface.
