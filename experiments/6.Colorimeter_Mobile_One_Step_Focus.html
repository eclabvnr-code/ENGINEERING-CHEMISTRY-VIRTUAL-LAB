Principle: Colorimetric Estimation of Copper

1. Complex Formation Reaction

Cupric ions (Cu²⁺) react with ammonium hydroxide (NH₄OH) in 1:1 solution to form a deep blue-violet tetraamminecopper(II) complex:

CuSO₄ + 4 NH₄OH ⟶ [Cu(NH₃)₄]SO₄ + 4 H₂O
Cu²⁺ + 4 NH₃ ⟶ [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺ (deep royal blue complex)

2. Beer-Lambert Law & Filter 61 Selection

The intensity of the blue color is directly proportional to copper concentration. The complex exhibits maximum absorption at λ_max ≈ 610 nm (orange-red spectrum). Hence, Filter number 61 is selected on the colorimeter.

A = OD = -log₁₀(I / I₀) = ε · c · l
Optical Density (OD) ∝ Concentration (c) ∝ Volume of CuSO₄ (V)

3. Quantitative Determination of Copper in Sample

From calibration curve: Unknown volume = V_x mL
Amount of Copper present in unknown sample = (V_x × Strength of Stock CuSO₄ × 63.546) / 249.68 g
